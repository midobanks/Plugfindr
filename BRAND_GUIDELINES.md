## 1. Brand foundation

### 1.1 What Plugfindr is [Supplied]

Plugfindr is a professional talent discovery and specialist-matching platform. It helps businesses discover, connect with and engage trusted independent specialists. It is **Nigeria-first, business-focused, trust-led and built to scale**.

### 1.2 The problem we solve [Supplied]

Businesses don't necessarily struggle to find people who offer services. They struggle to find the right people they can trust to deliver. Plugfindr bridges that gap.

### 1.3 Brand idea [Supplied]

Plugfindr is the resourceful person everyone knows: someone who understands the problem, knows where to look and can make the right introduction.

> _"Do you know someone who can help with this?"_
> Usually, we did. Now we do it at scale.

### 1.4 Positioning pillars [Supplied]

| Pillar     | Meaning                                              |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Discovery  | Make finding specialists easier                      |
| Relevance  | Match businesses with the right expertise            |
| Trust      | Make specialist selection more confident             |
| Speed      | Reduce time spent sourcing talent                    |
| Connection | Turn discovery into meaningful working relationships |

### 1.5 Audiences [Supplied]

- **Demand side (primary customer):** startups and founders, SMEs and growing businesses, corporate teams, agencies and consulting firms, entrepreneurs with project-based needs.
- **Supply side (network):** product designers, software developers, marketing specialists, business consultants, financial professionals, creative professionals, operations specialists.

Brand decisions default to the business audience first. Specialist-facing communication should still feel equally credible and respectful.

### 1.6 Brand personality [Supplied]

| Attribute    | Expression                                   |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Resourceful  | We know where to find the right people.      |
| Approachable | No unnecessary corporate language.           |
| Trustworthy  | Recommendations should be credible.          |
| Smart        | We understand what businesses actually need. |
| Efficient    | Less searching, more getting things done.    |
| Ambitious    | Built in Africa, ready for the world.        |

### 1.7 Brand belief [Supplied]

**Great talent deserves to be found.**

## 2. Messaging system [Supplied]

One hierarchy, no competing taglines. Use the right line for the right job.

| Purpose            | Message                                                | Where it lives                                              |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Master tagline     | **We know someone**                                    | Logo lockups, brand sign-offs, footers, brand-level moments |
| Campaign tagline   | **Find the right plug**                                | Campaigns, ads, social, launch activity                     |
| Product promise    | The right specialist, without the endless search       | Website hero support, product descriptions, onboarding      |
| Business message   | Whatever you need done, find someone who can do it     | Business acquisition, landing pages                         |
| Specialist message | Put your expertise in front of businesses that need it | Specialist recruitment and onboarding                       |
| Brand belief       | Great talent deserves to be found                      | Manifesto, About page, internal culture, recruitment        |

**Rules**

- Never invent new taglines for general use. Campaign-specific headlines are fine but must not replace the master or campaign tagline.
- "We know someone" is the sign-off. "Find the right plug" is the call to action.
- Don't stack taglines together in the same lockup.

## 3. Voice and tone

### 3.1 Voice principles [Supplied]

- **Conversational, but not childish.**
- **Confident, but not boastful.**
- **Nigerian-inspired, but globally understandable.**
- **Professional, without sounding unnecessarily corporate.**

### 3.2 Say it like this [Supplied]

| Instead of                                              | Say                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Discover our extensive database of service providers." | "Looking for someone who can get it done? Find your plug." |
| "Submit your service requirements."                     | "Tell us who you need."                                    |
| "Browse available professionals."                       | "Meet your next specialist."                               |

### 3.3 Working with "plug" [Recommended]

"Plug" is the brand's cultural hook: a trusted connection. Use it with care.

- **Do:** "Find your plug." "There's probably a plug for that." "Every business needs a good plug."
- **Do:** Use it to describe the _relationship_ (a trusted connection), not to label every person or feature.
- **Don't:** Overuse it in formal, legal, financial or compliance content (terms, verification notices, payments, safety).
- **Don't:** Let Nigerian slang become so dense that it excludes international readers. One local flavour per piece is enough.
- **Don't:** Use "plug" in ways that could be read as drug slang in global contexts. Keep context clearly professional ("find the right plug" paired with business or specialist language).

### 3.4 Tone by context [Recommended]

| Context                   | Tone                           | Example                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Marketing / homepage      | Warm, confident, quick         | "Looking for someone who can get it done? Find your plug."                  |
| Onboarding                | Encouraging, clear, short      | "Tell us who you need. A few quick questions and we'll take it from there." |
| Match results             | Helpful, specific, credible    | "Here are 4 specialists who've done this before. Here's why each one fits." |
| Verification              | Plain, reassuring, precise     | "We check, so you don't have to."                                           |
| Errors / empty states     | Calm, human, forward-moving    | "No plug for that yet. Tell us who you need and we'll look."                |
| Payments / legal / safety | Straightforward, minimal slang | "Funds are released when you approve the milestone."                        |
| Specialist communication  | Respectful, peer-level         | "Your work deserves to be seen by businesses that need it."                 |
| Social                    | Playful but sharp              | "Somebody asked 'do you know someone?' again. We do."                       |

### 3.5 Writing style [Recommended]

- Short sentences. Active voice. Plain words.
- Lead with the benefit or the next action.
- Use contractions ("we're", "you'll").
- Use "you" for the reader and "we" for Plugfindr.
- Sentence case for headlines and buttons ("Tell us who you need", not "Tell Us Who You Need").
- Write the brand name as **Plugfindr** in running text (capital P, one word). The logo itself is lowercase (see section 4).
- Use British English for Nigeria-first content (colour, organisation) unless a global campaign requires otherwise. Stay consistent within a piece.
- Currency: use the ₦ symbol or "NGN" with consistent formatting (₦250,000).

### 3.6 Words to favour and avoid [Recommended]

| Favour                                 | Avoid                                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| specialist, expert, independent talent | resource, vendor, service provider          |
| find, meet, connect, intro             | leverage, utilise, synergise                |
| tell us who you need                   | submit your requirements                    |
| trusted, verified, vetted (when true)  | best-in-class, world-class, guaranteed      |
| get it done                            | solutions, deliverables (unless contextual) |

Never claim "verified" or "vetted" unless the check has actually occurred and can be explained.

## 4. Logo

### 4.1 Overview [Supplied]

The Plugfindr logo is a horizontal lockup of a **symbol** and a **wordmark**.

**Symbol:** a green targeting reticle (a ring with four tick marks at the top, bottom, left and right) containing an **amber person icon** (head and shoulders). It expresses _finding the right person_.

**Wordmark:** the lowercase word **plugfindr** set in a bold geometric sans, split into two tones:

- **plug** in the dark tone (ink on light backgrounds, warm white on dark)
- **findr** in the green tone (brand green on light backgrounds, mint on dark)

The two-tone split makes "plug" and "find" read as the two ideas inside one name. Always keep the split.

### 4.2 Logo versions [Supplied]

| Version        | Use on                          | Symbol ring          | Person          | "plug"               | "findr"         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------- | -------------------- | --------------- |
| **Light mode** | White and light backgrounds     | Green `#0B6B3A`      | Amber `#F5A623` | Ink `#10231A`        | Green `#0B6B3A` |
| **Dark mode**  | Deep green and dark backgrounds | Warm white `#F7F5EF` | Amber `#F5A623` | Warm white `#F7F5EF` | Mint `#7BD3A0`  |

The amber person is constant in both versions. It is the brand's signature spark.

### 4.3 Clear space [Recommended]

Define **X** as half the height of the symbol. Keep at least **1X** of empty space on all sides of the lockup (nothing, including text, edges or other logos, may enter this area). On social avatars and tight placements, a minimum of **0.5X** is acceptable.

### 4.4 Minimum size [Recommended]

| Format               | Minimum width |
| -------------------- | ------------- |
| Full lockup, digital | 96 px         |
| Full lockup, print   | 25 mm         |
| Symbol only, digital | 16 px         |
| Symbol only, print   | 6 mm          |

Below the minimum lockup size, switch to the symbol alone.

### 4.5 Symbol-only use [Recommended]

The symbol can stand alone for app icons, favicons, avatars, stickers and small UI placements, once the full name is established nearby or the context is clear.

- Keep the amber person inside the ring with the four tick marks.
- On app icons, place the symbol on a **deep green `#0B3D26`** or **white** tile, centred, with generous padding.
- Do not use the symbol alone as the only identification in first-touch brand moments (ads, cold outreach); pair with the wordmark.

### 4.5.1 Wordmark-only use [Recommended]

Use the wordmark alone only in tight horizontal spaces (e.g. email signatures, narrow headers) where the symbol would be too small to read. Keep the plug/findr colour split.

### 4.6 Backgrounds [Recommended]

| Background                                                    | Logo to use                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| White, cream `#F7F5EF`, light neutrals                        | Light mode logo                                                                                                                                                   |
| Deep green `#0B3D26`, ink `#10231A`, other dark brand colours | Dark mode logo                                                                                                                                                    |
| Brand green `#0B6B3A`                                         | Dark mode logo, but check contrast on the "findr" mint (mint on brand green is ~3.7:1, so avoid for small sizes; prefer deep green or ink for small logos)        |
| Photography                                                   | Place on a calm area with a solid overlay or scrim. Choose the version with the stronger contrast. If neither achieves it, place the logo on a solid colour panel |
| Amber `#F5A623`                                               | Do not place the logo on amber                                                                                                                                    |
| Busy patterns or gradients                                    | Do not place the logo directly on them                                                                                                                            |

### 4.7 Logo don'ts

Do not:

- Recolour the logo, swap the green/amber roles, or recolour "plug" and "findr" to the same tone.
- Set "plugfindr" in capitals, title case or a different typeface.
- Separate "plug" and "findr" with a space, hyphen or line break (the name is one word, "Plugfindr" / "plugfindr").
- Stretch, skew, rotate, outline or add drop shadows, glows or bevels.
- Remove the tick marks from the ring or replace the person icon.
- Rearrange to stack the symbol above the wordmark without an approved stacked version (see Open items).
- Place the logo inside a container that crowds the clear space.
- Use the light mode logo on dark backgrounds or vice versa.
- Add the tagline into the lockup unless it is an approved tagline lockup (see 4.8).
- Use the JPG when an SVG is available.

### 4.8 Tagline lockup [Recommended]

"We know someone" may appear **beneath or beside** the logo in a lighter weight, sized at roughly the x-height of the wordmark, using Ink (light) or Warm White (dark). Maintain 1X between logo and tagline. A master lockup file should be produced and approved before wide use (see Open items).

### 4.9 Co-branding [Recommended]

When Plugfindr appears with partners, sponsors or clients: equal visual weight by height, separated by a thin vertical rule or ample space (minimum 2X), and Plugfindr never recoloured to match a partner. Specialists and businesses featured on Plugfindr should never appear to be endorsing the brand unless they have agreed.

## 5. Colour

### 5.1 Core palette [Supplied]

Hex values read directly from the logo files.

| Name             | Hex       | RGB           | Role                                                              |
| ---------------- | --------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Plug Green**   | `#0B6B3A` | 11, 107, 58   | Primary brand colour. Logo on light, primary buttons, key accents |
| **Ink**          | `#10231A` | 16, 35, 26    | Primary text on light, "plug" on light mode                       |
| **Deep Green**   | `#0B3D26` | 11, 61, 38    | Dark mode background, dark panels, hero backgrounds               |
| **Mint**         | `#7BD3A0` | 123, 211, 160 | "findr" on dark, highlights on dark backgrounds                   |
| **Signal Amber** | `#F5A623` | 245, 166, 35  | The person icon. Accent, highlights, attention                    |
| **Warm White**   | `#F7F5EF` | 247, 245, 239 | Dark-mode logo and text, soft light backgrounds                   |
| **White**        | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Light mode logo backing, cards, surfaces                          |

### 5.2 Colour roles and proportion [Recommended]

Aim for approximately:

- **60%** neutrals: White, Warm White, Ink / Deep Green (depending on mode)
- **30%** greens: Plug Green, Deep Green, Mint
- **10%** amber: a small, deliberate accent

Amber is the _spark_, the "person found". Use it sparingly: a highlight, a badge, a key icon, one underlined word. Do not use amber as a large background fill or body text colour.

### 5.3 Extended tints and states [Recommended]

Derived from the core palette for UI and layout. Confirm with design before production.

| Token       | Hex       | Use                                           |
| ----------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| `green-50`  | `#E8F3EC` | Subtle green backgrounds, selected rows       |
| `green-100` | `#CFE6D8` | Light tags, hover on light                    |
| `green-700` | `#095A31` | Hover/pressed for Plug Green                  |
| `ink-700`   | `#3B4A42` | Secondary text on light                       |
| `ink-500`   | `#66756D` | Tertiary text, placeholders (large text only) |
| `line`      | `#DCE3DE` | Borders and dividers on light                 |
| `mint-100`  | `#D9F2E4` | Success background on light                   |
| `amber-100` | `#FDF0D4` | Highlight / warning background                |

Semantic colours (for product UI): success uses green family, warning uses amber family, error uses a red such as `#C62828` (tune to harmonise), info uses a neutral blue-green or ink. Never rely on colour alone to convey state.

### 5.5 Colour pairings

**Recommended**

- Plug Green + White + Ink with a pinch of Amber (default light system)
- Deep Green + Warm White + Mint with Amber accents (default dark system)
- Warm White backgrounds with Plug Green headlines for a warmer, editorial feel

**Avoid**

- Amber + Plug Green as text/background pair
- Mint on white or Warm White for text (insufficient contrast)
- Introducing new brand-level hues (reds, blues, purples) in marketing without approval

### 5.6 Dark and light mode [Supplied/Recommended]

The brand ships **light** and **dark** logo versions. Mirror this in all product and marketing surfaces:

## 6. Typography

> The wordmark is drawn artwork (outlined paths), so no brand typeface is embedded in the files. The recommendations below are chosen to complement its bold, rounded-geometric character. If a typeface was used to design the wordmark, replace these with it.

### 6.1 Typeface recommendation [Recommended]

| Role                | Typeface                                                    | Why                                                                                                | Fallback                |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Headlines and UI    | **Plus Jakarta Sans** (SemiBold / Bold / ExtraBold)         | Friendly geometric sans, confident, well-suited to the wordmark's character, free via Google Fonts | Inter, system-ui, Arial |
| Body and long text  | **Inter** (Regular / Medium)                                | Highly legible at small sizes and on low-resolution screens                                        | system-ui, Arial        |
| Numbers, data, code | **Inter** with tabular numerals, or JetBrains Mono for code | Clean figures in tables, pricing and metrics                                                       | monospace               |

Support for Latin extended characters (including Yoruba tonal marks such as ẹ, ọ, ṣ and combining diacritics) must be tested before locking font choices, since the platform plans for Nigerian languages. If a chosen font fails on diacritics, select an alternative that supports them.

### 6.2 Type scale [Recommended]

| Style   | Size / line height                     | Weight         | Use                            |
| ------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Display | 56 / 60 px (desktop), 36 / 40 (mobile) | ExtraBold      | Hero headlines                 |
| H1      | 40 / 46                                | Bold           | Page titles                    |
| H2      | 30 / 36                                | Bold           | Section titles                 |
| H3      | 22 / 28                                | SemiBold       | Subsections, card titles       |
| Body L  | 18 / 28                                | Regular        | Intro paragraphs               |
| Body    | 16 / 24                                | Regular        | Default                        |
| Small   | 14 / 20                                | Regular/Medium | Metadata, captions             |
| Micro   | 12 / 16                                | Medium         | Labels, badges (use sparingly) |

### 6.3 Typographic rules [Recommended]

- Sentence case for headlines, buttons and labels. Reserve all caps for tiny labels with added letter-spacing.
- Left-align body text. Keep line length to 60 to 80 characters.
- Use bold weights in headlines and keep body weights light to preserve hierarchy.
- Highlight a single key word in a headline with **Plug Green** (light) or **Mint** (dark), mirroring the "plug / findr" split. Use amber underline accents only on dark backgrounds or with sufficient contrast.
- Do not use more than two typefaces on a page.
- Minimum 16 px body text on mobile web.

## 7. Iconography and graphic elements

### 7.1 The reticle as a brand device [Supplied/Recommended]

The reticle (ring with four ticks) is the core graphic idea: _targeting the right person._ It can be used as a recurring device:

- **Reticle frame:** crop profile photos or highlight a match with a thin ring and four ticks.
- **Focus marks:** use the four ticks as corner or edge markers to bracket a headline or card.
- **Pattern:** a low-contrast, repeated reticle pattern on Deep Green backgrounds (keep opacity low so it never competes with content).
- **Loader:** a ticking or pulsing ring for "finding your match" states.

Always keep the amber person as the single point of colour inside the reticle when the device is used as a literal logo reference.

### 7.2 UI icon style [Recommended]

- Outline icons, ~2 px stroke at 24 px, round caps and joins (consistent with the symbol's 4.56-unit stroke and rounded ticks).
- Single colour (Ink, Plug Green, or Warm White on dark); amber only for a single highlighted state.
- Suggested libraries: Lucide or Phosphor (regular weight), customised to match stroke and corner treatment.
- Avoid filled, 3D or multi-colour icon sets.

### 7.3 Shapes and corners [Recommended]

- Rounded corners echo the logo's friendly geometry: 8 px for inputs and chips, 12 to 16 px for cards, 20 px for hero panels, fully rounded for pills and avatars.
- The logo files themselves use a 20-unit rounded card corner (on a 420 × 340 artboard).

### 7.4 Badges [Recommended]

Verification and trust badges are central to the product. Design rules:

- Simple pill or shield shapes using Plug Green (or Mint on dark) with Ink/Warm White text.
- Pair every badge with a text label ("Identity verified", "Portfolio reviewed"). Never use icon-only trust marks.
- Reserve **Amber** for the top-tier or "proven" badge, so it retains meaning.

## 8. Imagery and illustration

### 8.1 Photography direction [Recommended]

Plugfindr imagery should feel **real, capable and local**.

- **Subjects:** independent specialists at work (designers, developers, accountants, consultants, creatives) and the business owners who hire them, shown in believable settings: offices, co-working spaces, homes, studios, markets, sites.
- **Representation:** predominantly Nigerian and African professionals, with room for global diversity as the brand expands. Avoid stock imagery that feels foreign or generic.
- **Mood:** confident, focused, collaborative. Real moments (introductions, handshakes, screens, sketches, conversation) over staged posing.
- **Light and colour:** natural light, warm tones, rich greens and soft neutrals where possible; avoid heavy filters that distort skin tones.
- **Composition:** leave clean areas for text and logo placement. Use the reticle frame as an optional overlay for "found" moments.
- **Ethics:** use real specialists only with written consent; verify that testimonials, names and results are genuine.

### 8.2 Avoid

- Cliché stock (handshake silhouettes, glowing networks, robots, people pointing at floating icons).
- Images implying unrealistic wealth or hustle culture clichés.
- Imagery that stereotypes or exoticises Africa.
- Heavy gradients, neon, or visual styles unrelated to the palette.

### 8.3 Illustration and graphics [Recommended]

- Flat, geometric shapes using the brand palette, built from circles, rings, rounded rectangles and the reticle motif.
- Person icons (head and shoulders) can be used as simple building blocks for network or matching diagrams. Keep the amber person as the "found" element and others in green or neutral.
- Data visualisation: Plug Green for primary series, Mint/green tints for secondary, Ink for text, Amber for highlight only.

## 9. Layout and spacing

### 9.1 Grid and spacing [Recommended]

- 12-column grid on desktop (max content width ~1200 px), 4-column on mobile.
- 8 px spacing base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96.
- Generous whitespace: the brand promise is _less searching, more getting things done_, so layouts should feel uncluttered.

### 9.2 Composition principles [Recommended]

1. **One idea per section.** One headline, one supporting line, one clear action.
2. **Human first.** Lead with people (specialists, business owners) and outcomes before features.
3. **Show the trust.** Place verification and ratings close to decision points.
4. **Left-aligned, ragged-right text** with clear visual hierarchy.
5. **Amber as spark.** One amber moment per view, at most two.

### 9.3 Buttons and calls to action [Recommended]

| Type                         | Light mode                                   | Dark mode                         |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Primary                      | Plug Green background, White text            | Mint background, Deep Green text  |
| Secondary                    | White background, Plug Green border and text | Transparent, Mint border and text |
| Accent (rare, high-priority) | Amber background, Ink text                   | Amber background, Ink text        |
| Text link                    | Plug Green, underlined on hover              | Mint, underlined on hover         |

Primary CTA language: "Tell us who you need", "Find your plug", "Meet your specialists", "Join as a specialist".

## 10. UI and product expression

### 10.1 Principles [Recommended]

- **Clarity over cleverness.** The product is about confidence; interfaces should be self-explanatory.
- **Trust is visible.** Verification badges, ratings, response times and "why this match" should be prominent on results and profile cards.
- **Short paths.** Reduce steps between need and shortlist. Use progressive disclosure for forms.
- **Mobile-first.** Many users will arrive on mid-range Android devices and variable connections. Keep pages light, tap targets generous (minimum 44 px) and layouts resilient.
- **WhatsApp-native moments.** Notification and share assets should work in WhatsApp (square and vertical formats, strong contrast, minimal text).

### 10.2 Component notes [Recommended]

- **Specialist card:** photo (rounded), name, headline, top skills (chips), location, rate "from", badges, rating, availability. Primary action: "Request intro".
- **Match card:** includes a **"Why this match"** block of 2 to 3 plain-language reasons.
- **Verification badge:** pill with icon and label, tappable to explain what was checked.
- **Request form:** conversational prompts in brand voice, one question group per screen on mobile, progress indicator.
- **Empty state:** friendly message plus an action, e.g. "No plug for that yet. Tell us who you need."
- **Loading state:** reticle animation with copy such as "Finding your plug…"

### 10.3 Form and input styling [Recommended]

- Inputs: 48 px height, 8 px radius, 1 px `line` border, Plug Green focus ring (2 px) with sufficient contrast.
- Error text: red with an icon and plain-language instruction, not colour alone.
- Labels above fields. Placeholder text is a hint, never a label.

---

## 11. Motion

### 11.1 Principles [Recommended]

Motion should feel quick, purposeful and light, mirroring "less searching, more getting things done."

- **Duration:** 150 to 250 ms for UI transitions; up to 400 ms for larger reveals.
- **Easing:** ease-out for entrances, ease-in for exits; avoid bouncy or elastic effects.
- **Signature motion:** the reticle "locks on" (a ring tightening around the person icon, ticks snapping in) for match-found moments and loading.
- **Restraint:** no looping decorative animation near reading content. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and provide static alternatives.

## 13. Accessibility

- Meet **WCAG 2.1 AA** at minimum for contrast, focus, target size and text scaling (section 5.4 provides computed ratios).
- Never use amber text on white. Use Ink, Plug Green or `amber-700` instead.
- Do not rely on colour alone (green/amber) to show verification level or status. Pair with text and icons.
- All images need meaningful alt text; the logo's alt text is "Plugfindr".
- Provide captions and transcripts for video and audio.
- Support keyboard navigation and visible focus states throughout.
- Test with Nigerian-language characters and with system text scaling up to 200%.
- Respect reduced motion preferences.
